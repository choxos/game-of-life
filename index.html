          How it works

          Years ahead

          We use remaining life expectancy, written e(x): the average number of years still ahead for people who have already reached your age in your country. It comes from the United Nations World Population Prospects 2024 life tables for 2026, medium variant, by single year of age. Tools that subtract your age from life expectancy at birth undercount, because having reached your age already improves the odds.

          It is an average, not a forecast for you. These period tables assume today’s death rates hold steady; if they keep falling, your real number is likely a little higher.

          Time that is yours

          Waking hours, minus work and commuting until the age you stop working, minus daily upkeep for the rest of your life. A work year is 52 weeks minus your time off. You decide which parts of your day count as obligations.

          Conversions

          • Reading: adults read English silently at about 238 words a minute for nonfiction and 260 for fiction (Brysbaert, 2019, a meta-analysis of 190 studies). A typical novel runs about 80,000 words.
          • Languages: about 500 to 600 guided learning hours from beginner to B2 (Cambridge English). A time equivalence, not a promise.
          • Trips count 8 free hours a day. Movies are 2 hours, TV seasons 8.
          • Conversions in a row are alternatives. An hour can only be spent once.
